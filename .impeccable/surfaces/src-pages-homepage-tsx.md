# Surface: homepage (`/`)

Mode: Persuade. Brand story + product teaser. Not a technical How-it-works page.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The homepage sells the turn from a lived experience to sourced knowledge. It refuses a syllabus, a chatbot, and a split-hero SaaS template.

OWN-WORLD: Cinematic knowledge + modern Arabic editorial. Soft off-white ground, forest ink as accent only, architectural light, layered translucent planes, large Arabic type. No notebook, pen, olive, forest stock, people, or SaaS cards.

STORY: A first-timer understands what طمأنينة is, why starting without a topic name is allowed, that knowledge comes from sources, and then starts.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Folio `01 / البداية`. Brand `طمأنينة`. Dominant slogan. Supporting `اكتشف الإسلام من حيث تبدأ أنت.` Full-bleed architectural light still. No content CTA.

FORM: Scroll structure from `.impeccable/mocks/home-story.jpg`: ما هي طمأنينة؟ → ماذا تقدم طمأنينة؟ (one paper transforming through اكتب / اكتشف / تعلّم) → ما المشكلة التي تحلها؟ → كيف تتحول التجربة إلى معرفة؟ (same morph, once) → teaser `اكتب ما في بالك…` → one CTA `ابدأ الآن`. Seed: user-pinned after critique; no concept-seed.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Approved comps:
- Primary first viewport: `.impeccable/mocks/home-opening.jpg`
- Primary scroll: `.impeccable/mocks/home-story.jpg`
- How reference (not homepage syllabus): `.impeccable/mocks/how-learn.jpg`

Do not literalize: LTR arrows, two duplicate How rows, invented tafsir, 7-step journey on `/`.
