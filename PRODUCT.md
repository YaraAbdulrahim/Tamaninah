# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are people who want to learn about Islam but may not know the name of a topic or where to begin. That includes Muslims, people returning to or learning Islam, curious users, and non-Muslims who want to understand Islam.

They arrive with a feeling, a question, an experience, or something occupying them. They need الطمأنينة والوضوح at the start of a learning journey, without being assumed to already know the path.

## Product Purpose

طمأنينة | Tamaninah turns a lived experience or question into a personal learning journey about Islam.

Promise: «من تجربة تعيشها… إلى معرفة تكتشفها.» — landing line: «كل تجربة ممكن تكون بداية لاكتشاف الإسلام.»

Success is that someone can write what occupies them in their own words, discover a fitting starting point, and complete a clear path through source-backed Islamic content — without being diagnosed, judged, or treated as a fatwa or therapy client.

## Positioning

The core experience is الشعور + المعرفة. The product starts from the person as they are, then helps them discover a suitable starting point for learning about Islam.

The mechanism a generic Islamic site or chatbot cannot copy: flexible human input, AI that only understands and routes, and religious knowledge that never comes from the model.

Principle: «الذكاء يساعدك على الاكتشاف، والمعرفة تأتي من مصادرها.»

## Operating Context

Arabic-first, bilingual (Arabic and English) Vite/React web app. Entry is free writing (feeling, question, or preoccupation), then a guided learning journey.

Canonical journey:

من التجربة أو السؤال → فهم السياق → اكتشاف موضوعات → اختيار موضوع → ربط محايد بالسياق → رحلة تعلّم من محتوى إسلامي موثوق → نهاية واضحة

Shipped learning steps after topic choice:

context → topics → link → discover → quran_explanation [optional] → hadith [optional] → hadith_explanation [optional] → story [optional] → end

There is no reflection step, no separate story explanation, and no placeholder religious content. Optional steps appear only when verified catalog content exists.

The project is also an entry for the Global AI Challenge in Service of Islamic Content.

## Capabilities and Constraints

**Input is flexible. Religious output is source-constrained.**

AI may:

- understand what the user wrote
- summarize context
- suggest topics
- make a neutral contextual link
- order a starting point

AI is not a religious authority. AI must not generate Quran, Hadith, Seerah, religious explanations, rulings, references, or sources. Those come only from approved, reviewed, source-backed catalog content, shown with their sources.

Tamaninah is not a fatwa platform, not a therapy platform, and does not diagnose or judge the user. Safety interrupts must send a person in danger toward human help rather than continuing the journey.

Do not invent testimonials, statistics, or claims. Do not fill gaps with fake religious text.

Open: which catalog items ship for every theme; live-model configuration in some environments.

## Brand Commitments

Name: طمأنينة | Tamaninah.

Voice and presence: calm, trustworthy, modern, intelligent, human, premium, distinctive.

The product must feel like a modern knowledge experience, not a chatbot.

Binding visual constraint (not a designed system): modern Arabic editorial + premium digital learning + cinematic storytelling + sophisticated typography + subtle natural visual language. Avoid generic AI SaaS, chatbot aesthetics, generic Islamic-website look, overly traditional visual language, and excessive decoration.

## Evidence on Hand

- Product copy (Arabic and English) in `src/copy.ts`; the single page in `src/App.tsx`.
- Experience states (topic choice, learning journey, knowledge answer, referral, insufficient reference, unclear,
  error) in `src/ExperienceViews.tsx`; the browser client for `POST /api/understand` in `src/understand.ts`.
- Verified content catalog, provenance, source registry, level guards and the Qur'an snapshot on the server
  (`server/content/**`).
- Approved knowledge — the `sources.pdf` transcription and the Bayyinat question index with verified excerpts — in
  `server/content/knowledge/**`.
- Measured behaviour on the PDF's safety cases and extra cases: `docs/EVALUATION.md` (generated by `npm run eval`).
- No customer testimonials, press, or usage statistics. Future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Start from the person as they write — no required term, syllabus, or prior knowledge.
2. AI discovers and routes; Islamic knowledge comes only from its sources.
3. Every religious surface is catalog-backed or it does not appear.
4. Calm and clarity at the start of learning; never diagnosis, fatwa, or therapy.
5. End the journey clearly; do not invent proof, numbers, or religious filler.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Arabic is the default language and a first-class RTL experience; English is a parallel LTR experience, not a fallback. Copy should remain usable for people who do not already know Islamic terminology.
