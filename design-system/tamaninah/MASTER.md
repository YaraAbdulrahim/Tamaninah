# Tamaninah — Living Editorial (MASTER)

## Concept
Premium Arabic-first digital publication for knowledge discovery. Not AI/SaaS/institutional template.

## Colors
- Background: `#F4F5F2`
- Ink: `#102019`
- Muted: `#59645D`
- Accent: `#1E4A3A`
- Rule: `#C8CFC9`
- Surface: `#FFFFFF`

## Typography
- Display (AR): El Messiri
- Body (AR): IBM Plex Sans Arabic
- Hierarchy: DISPLAY → H1 → H2 → BODY → LABEL → META
- Chapter numbers: `01` `02` `03` as anchors

## Layout
- Editorial columns, thin rules, asymmetric splits
- No fullscreen video background
- No liquid glass / card grids
- Images as cropped editorial blocks only

## Motion
- Restrained: opacity, slight translate, sticky chapter reveals
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: disable transitions

## Components
- Flat nav + hairline border, scroll compact
- Solid accent buttons, 6px radius
- Surfaces only where interaction requires containment

## Avoid
- Purple, neon, black sections, chatbot UI, spinner-heavy loading
