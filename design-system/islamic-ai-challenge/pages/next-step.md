# Practical next step — page spec

**Authority:** `docs/PRD.md` §23 · Flow: `UX-STRUCTURE.md` §9

After the story and the reflection, the companion may offer a step. **An offer, never an instruction.**

---

## Not

A command · a fatwa · a diagnosis · a checklist · a habit tracker · a streak · a gamified goal · a prescription.

## May be

Reading part of the Qur'an · duʿāʾ · prayer · ṣadaqah · talking to someone trusted · taking time to rest · thinking through a practical step · seeking a professional when needed · learning more about the story.

## Phrasing

Conditional and unhurried:

> "إذا يناسبك، ممكن اليوم تبدأ بخطوة بسيطة: اقرأ قليلًا من القرآن وخذ وقتك بدون ما تضغط على نفسك."

## Composition

One suggestion at a time, in the companion's voice, visually separate from the source. Always dismissible, and declining leads back into the conversation. Must be contextual to what the person actually said.
