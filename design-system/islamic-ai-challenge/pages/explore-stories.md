# Withdrawn — explore by topic or value

**Authority:** `docs/PRD.md` §7, §29

There is no `/explore`, no topic index, no stories library, and no "choose a value" entry anywhere in the product. Any of them would invert the product (content → user instead of user → content).

Continuation after a journey is specified in `journey-completion.md`: a new experience, the paths already discovered in this journey, or deeper into the source of the story just heard.
