# Story — `/story`

**Authority:** `docs/PRD.md` §17, §18, §20 · Flow: `UX-STRUCTURE.md` §7

Entered from the conversation, after the person has agreed to hear it.

---

## Climate

Dusk for the beats. Meaning and source move back toward daylight.

## Beats — not an article

| Beat | |
|---|---|
| 01 | البداية — ما حدث وفق المصدر |
| 02 | الموقف — التحدي أو الظرف |
| 03 | ما حدث بعد ذلك — النتيجة أو التعامل |

One beat per viewport. Progressive disclosure, focused typography, `01 / 03` indicator, Previous · Next, and **source reachable from every beat**.

## Why this story

Before or with beat 01, the connection to the conversation is stated without overclaiming:

> "اخترت لك هذه القصة لأن فيها جانبًا قريبًا من الشيء الذي وصفته لي."

Never "this is exactly your situation".

## Narrative style

Warm, clear, human, close to the person, never stiff, never preachy.

Closeness of tone is allowed in the introduction, transitions, explanation, reflection, and dialogue. **Qur'an, ḥadīth, original texts, and quotations stay exactly as the source has them**, visually distinct from the companion's voice.

## Content law

Verified sources only. Never invent events, dialogue, attributed sayings, unverified ḥadīth, incorrect verses, dramatic detail the source does not carry, or a story stitched from separate events. No figurative depiction of the Prophet ﷺ or other sacred figures.

Each beat is tied to its source. If a beat is not in the source, say so.

## After the last beat

Return to the conversation — never a dead end and never an automatic "next article".

## Motion

Scene transitions and slight depth. No scroll-jacking, skip always available, reduced motion swaps instantly.
