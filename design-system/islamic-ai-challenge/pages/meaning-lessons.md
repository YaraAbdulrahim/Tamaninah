# Meaning layer — page spec

**Authority:** `docs/PRD.md` §22, §28 · Flow: `UX-STRUCTURE.md` §9

Reached **after** the story, out of the conversation — never as a lesson delivered to the person.

---

## Climate

Dusk easing back to daylight.

## Three things that must never merge

### Source
What the source actually says.

> هذا ما ورد في المصدر.

### Connection
How the system sees a relation to this person's experience. Hedged, never asserted as fact.

> قد يكون الجانب الأقرب لتجربتك هو…

### Reflection
Questions or thoughts to sit with. Questions only.

> ماذا يمكن أن يتغير في نظرتك للموقف لو تذكرت هذا المعنى؟

Each is visually distinct and labelled in plain language. The person must always be able to tell apart what the source said, what the AI said, and what is only a reflection.

## Built from the person's answer

The meaning follows what the person said stood out to them in the story — bounded by the source, with nothing added beyond it.

## Forbidden

Fatwa tone · "you must" · personalised religious rulings · a diagnosis · three-card "key takeaways" · collapsing the three voices into one AI paragraph.

## Then

Continue to the verified source, or to a practical next step, or back into the conversation. The person chooses.
