# Retired — context understanding as a screen

**Authority:** `docs/PRD.md` §11

There is no `/understanding` screen and no screen that displays the system's reading of the person as data.

`Detected values: Patience, Hope, Trust` and any equivalent (theme chips, scores, pipeline views) are **forbidden** — they turn a human experience into cold machine analysis.

Context understanding is internal. It may surface only as human language inside the conversation:

> "أفهم أنك مو بس خايف من المستقبل، أنت متعب من عدم معرفتك وش بيصير."

See `conversation.md`.
