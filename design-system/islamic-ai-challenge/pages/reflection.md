## Climate

Daylight. Still.

## Frame

**ما جئت به → ما اكتشفته → ما يمكنك أن تتأمله**

The person's original words return, alongside the story they discovered.

## Rules

- Prompts are questions, never duties. Never "you must".
- Optional writing space. Leaving it empty is fine.
- Warmth without overclaiming: no promise that the feeling will pass, no diagnosis, no ruling.
- Distinct from the source: a reflection is labelled as a reflection.

## Then

A practical next step if it fits, or back into the conversation, or `/continue`.
