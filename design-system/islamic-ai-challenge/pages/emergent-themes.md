# Retired — values / meaning discovery as a screen

**Authority:** `docs/PRD.md` §3, §7, §11, §12

The screen where derived Islamic values emerged and the person chose one is **withdrawn**.

The product does not ask the person to pick "الصبر" or "التوكل", does not show a values list anywhere, and has no global fixed list visible to the user.

What replaces it: **conversation**. The companion understands the context, offers a story with consent, and the meaning is built after the story from what the person says.

See `conversation.md` and `UX-STRUCTURE.md` §5.
